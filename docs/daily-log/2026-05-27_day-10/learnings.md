# 📚 Learnings

## Kafka Broker

Kafka broker stores:
- topics
- partitions
- offsets
- event streams

---

## Producer

Producer publishes events into Kafka topics.

---

## Consumer

Consumer continuously streams events from Kafka.

---

## Topic

Topic acts as event category or stream.

Example:
payment-events

---

## Partition

Partitions enable horizontal scaling and parallelism.

---

## Consumer Group

Consumers within same group share work across partitions.

---

## Offset

Kafka tracks consumer reading progress using offsets.

---

## Massive Insight

Kafka stores facts about what happened over time.
